fix(stamina): the spend goal stops claiming free stamina, and stops re-tapping a dead control

Two live measurements, one goal whose meter has to go *down*.

**The spend goal was feeding itself.**  `AVOID_STAMINA_WASTE` declares three ways to spend
stamina and only the beast one carries a block, so the runtime rides the intel flow by
setting `current_goal = "INTEL"`.  But the intel route claims the free stamina gift, which
*raises* the number the spend goal exists to lower: measured 2026-09-19, the spend goal's
runs were consumed by `OPEN_STAMINA_SOURCES -> CLAIM_FREE_STAMINA`, the no-progress rule
then deferred the goal for 30 minutes, and it came back and did the same thing.  Stamina
sat at 547 throughout.

The two goals now have separate route names.  `RuleBrain._intel_like` is the one place that
decides they share the intel flow, and the five `current_goal == "INTEL"` sites that
genuinely mean "working the intel missions" go through it -- including the Hero-Journey
camp card and the squad-setup page, which are the same pages for both goals.  The
free-stamina claim keeps its place for every other goal and is refused only for the one
where a rising number is the wrong direction.

**The free control can be dead while still looking live.**  Bounded live probe
2026-09-19T05:17Z (`popup OPEN_MAP_BACK`): 丰盛的招待's 领取 control is still drawn and still
matches its template at distance 0, but a tap on its centre changes nothing -- the panel
returns to the same state, so `FREE_STAMINA_CLAIM_NOT_PROVEN` is the honest reading.  Eight
consecutive runs proved it was not transient, and the brain re-decided the claim from the
frame alone every cycle, wedging four goals in a row (05:07Z..05:15Z).

`StaminaSupplyStore` now records the refusal where the positive countdown already lives --
the panel is the only place either is observable -- and `claim_cooling_down()` holds the
next attempt for 30 minutes.  A bounded retry, not a surrender: the gift is still attempted,
just not once per cycle, and the supply cadence is hours so a fresh gift cannot plausibly be
skipped.  Absent means False, so an unknown refusal never suppresses a claim that was never
attempted.  `_write` merges rather than replaces, because the supply instant and the refusal
are independent facts learned on different visits and an overwrite silently dropped one.

**Not yet live-verified.**  The route split passes its offline tests and `check_wiring`, but
the goal it serves is still unmet: stamina has not come down under the acceptance line.  The
next measurement is the one that counts -- whether `AVOID_STAMINA_WASTE` now rides
`SPEND_STAMINA` without claiming free stamina and whether the number falls.
