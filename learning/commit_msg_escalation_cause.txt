fix(escalation): the queue must not restate a cause the gate already measured

``_no_progress_reason`` exists to stop one specific lie, and its own docstring says which: the
sentence "every step passed its own verifier" is true only of the case it was written for
(2026-09-18: 58 beast scans that landed and moved nothing).  A goal also fails to advance when the
executor never issued an action, and then no verifier judged anything -- "so the claim was false in
the ledger".  That fix reads the episode counts and says which of three things happened.

**The fix landed in the gate and left this copy behind.**  ``candidates_from_run`` builds its own
sentence for the same deferral, in its own words, with the same unconditional half::

    f"{goal} was attempted {streak} time(s) in a row and no part of it advanced, "
    f"while every step passed its own verifier: the route is running but it cannot reach the goal."

and the measured sentence is sitting in the very row it iterates -- ``deferral["reason"]``, written
by the gate from the episode counts.  One fact, two spellings, and the second one unmeasured.

Measured 2026-10-09 over ``learning/workbuddy_escalations.jsonl``: **6,516 records carry the
unmeasured sentence**, across 14 goals.  343 of them name a goal that has **no episode at all** in
the retained stream, so whether any step passed a verifier cannot be read back even in principle::

    ALLIANCE_ROUTINE          327 claims,   0 episodes
    USE_FREE_ARENA_ATTEMPTS    11 claims,   0 episodes
    LABYRINTH_DAILY             4 claims,   0 episodes
    EVENT_MINIMUM_GUARANTEE     1 claim,    0 episodes

The last two are the goals this project deliberately emits as ``prepared_only`` with
``available_skills=()``, precisely so the scheduler will not walk them by guessed controls -- they
have never run.  A development agent was nonetheless handed a measurement saying they had been
"attempted N time(s) in a row" with every step passing its verifier.

The fix uses the deferral's own measured cause and keeps the escalation's closing sentence, which is
a policy statement about what a repeated wall means rather than a measurement and is therefore this
function's to make.  A deferral that carries no reason at all -- an older row -- now falls back to
saying what is actually known ("refused by the scheduler for N consecutive attempt(s)") and claims
nothing about verifiers.

**Tests.**  Two cases in ``NoGoalProgressDeferralTest``.  The bite is shown rather than claimed:
restoring the unconditional sentence turns ``test_the_cause_is_the_deferrals_own_measured_sentence``
red with the old text quoted back, and leaves the other 96 in that file green.  The second case is a
negative control for the new fallback path -- it passes in both arms, which is stated here rather
than implied.  A/B over the seven suites that touch this module: **12 failed / 200 passed before,
11 failed / 201 passed after**, and the eleven that remain are byte-identical in both arms.

**Not verified live.**  AUTO is running (restarted by the operator's instruction at 01:29 local), but
this changes a *reason string* on the escalation path, which fires at the end of a run whose goal
deferred -- so the first observation is the next such run, not a test.
