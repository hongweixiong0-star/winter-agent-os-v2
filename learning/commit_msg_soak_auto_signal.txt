docs(handoff): the soak cannot see AUTO running, and the reason is its sampling

AUTO reaches AUTO_RUNNING with both threads alive -- independently observed twice in 45 seconds by
polling the snapshot every 2 s, at 12:28:20 and 12:29:01.  But the soak's own 24 samples over 4.91
minutes recorded auto_running False on every single one, so its auto_gameplay condition failed with
"窗口内测到 AUTO 未在运行（AUTO 状态可读但为停止）".

That sentence is wrong, and the arithmetic says why.  Each round is a fresh process, and the thread
flags are true only while the machine is mid-round -- measured at roughly 4 seconds inside a 30-40 s
round, so about a tenth of the time.  The soak samples from the probe thread on a fixed cadence of
roughly 12 seconds.  Its aggregation is already lenient (any sample true wins), but a tenth-of-the-time
signal sampled a twelfth as often is a coin toss that it never lands, and the two periodicities can sit
near a ratio that keeps the sample in the same phase of the round.

So the check is unreliable by construction rather than merely unlucky, and it is the one condition
still failing.  I have NOT changed it: what "AUTO continued gameplay" should mean is an acceptance
criterion, and quietly widening a condition that is failing is exactly what this project forbids.  The
honest fix is for the operator to decide, and the shape of it is to judge continuity across the window
-- rounds that actually happened -- instead of sampling a per-round thread flag and hoping to catch it.

The rest of the soak's conditions are healthy on the same 24 samples: gui_alive, gateway_reachable
(24/24 = 100%), single_gateway, port_owner_legal, no_restart_loop, no_duplicate_job,
no_extra_spawn_per_refresh, no_black_console all pass.  pump_ticking fails on one stale heartbeat out
of 24, which is the same "sampled between rounds" shape.

Recorded, not fixed: the acceptance condition needs a decision, not a patch.
